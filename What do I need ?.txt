What do I need ?


1- 0.6mm Wood panels (however much you need to cut every part out)
2- Access to a laser cutter for the Wood panels
3- A raspberry pi 3/4 A/B So long as it has enough ram to run the software and enough I/O ports
4- 4x 90 degree bent aluminium pieces for the legs
5- https://a.co/d/1CEWdJr  IR webcam
6- Sealant (any type works, so long as it keeps the wood from getting wet and rotting)
7-Any sort of generic screwed locking mechanism which you can lock with a lock (for the electronics service door)
8- knob (for the electronics service door)
9- any 12 volt battery, so long as they don't exceed the dimensions of the battery compartment
10- https://a.co/d/bGvNEJu 12v/24v to usb c adapter
11- access to a soldering iron and solder for the adapter
12- Any generic LED's and jumper cables
13- drill (highly optional)
14- Sim card with lte/5g and sms
15- sim card module:https://www.seeedstudio.com/LTE-CAT-4-EG25-GL-HAT-for-Raspberry-Pi-p-6325.html
16- motor (undecided for now)
17- And finally but not least, FOOD !!!!
