What Do I Need?
----------------

Materials & Components:

1. 0.6 mm wood panels — enough to laser-cut all required parts
2. Access to a laser cutter
3. Raspberry Pi 3 or 4 (Model A or B) — must have enough RAM and I/O ports
4. 4× 90-degree bent aluminum pieces for the legs
5. IR webcam — https://a.co/d/1CEWdJr
6. Wood sealant — anything that prevents moisture/rot
7. Generic screw-locking mechanism for the electronics service door
8. Knob for the electronics service door
9. 12 V battery — must fit inside the battery compartment
10. 12V/24V to USB-C adapter — https://a.co/d/bGvNEJu
11. Soldering iron and solder — for wiring the adapter
12. Generic LEDs and jumper wires
13. Drill (optional)
14. SIM card with LTE/5G and SMS
15. SIM card module — LTE Cat-4 EG25-GL HAT: https://www.seeedstudio.com/LTE-CAT-4-EG25-GL-HAT-for-Raspberry-Pi-p-6325.html
16. Motor (type undecided)
17. Food (very important)
